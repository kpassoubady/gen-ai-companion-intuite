# TurboTax: Small Business Deductions

Source: TurboTax Help (mock data for training)

Common small business deductions include the home office deduction, business
mileage, health insurance premiums for the self-employed, and the cost of
software and subscriptions used for work.

For the home office deduction, the simplified method lets you deduct 5 dollars
per square foot of office space, up to 300 square feet, for a maximum of 1500
dollars. The regular method deducts the actual percentage of home expenses used
for business.

Business mileage is deducted at the standard mileage rate used in this course,
which is 67 cents per mile. Keep a log with the date, purpose, and miles for
each trip. You cannot use both the standard mileage rate and actual vehicle
expenses for the same vehicle in the same year.
