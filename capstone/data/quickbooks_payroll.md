# QuickBooks: Payroll

Source: QuickBooks Help (mock data for training)

QuickBooks Payroll runs employee payroll and files payroll taxes. There are two
plans in this course: Core at 45 dollars per month plus 6 dollars per employee
per month, and Premium at 80 dollars per month plus 9 dollars per employee per
month.

To run payroll, go to Payroll, then Employees, then Run payroll. Enter hours for
hourly employees, review the amounts, and submit. With direct deposit turned on,
employees are paid two business days after you submit.

Premium includes same-day direct deposit and automated tax filing in all
supported states. Core files federal taxes automatically and supports state
filing in a limited set of states. Year-end W-2 forms are prepared for you on
both plans.
