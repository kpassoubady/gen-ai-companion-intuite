# QuickBooks: Expenses and Receipts

Source: QuickBooks Help (mock data for training)

You can track expenses by connecting a bank or credit card account, or by
entering them by hand. Connected accounts import new transactions once a day.
Go to Transactions, then Bank transactions to review and categorize them.

To capture a receipt, use the QuickBooks mobile app and take a photo, or forward
the receipt email to your receipts inbox. QuickBooks reads the vendor, date, and
amount and suggests a category. You review and confirm before it posts.

Categorizing expenses correctly matters at tax time because categories map to
tax lines. Common categories include Office supplies, Travel, Meals, and
Contractor payments. Contractor payments over 600 dollars in a year require a
1099 form.
