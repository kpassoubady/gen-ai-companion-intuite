# Account and Billing

Source: Intuit Account Help (mock data for training)

Manage your subscription from Settings, then Account and settings, then Billing
and subscription. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Changes
take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Billing is monthly by default. Switching to annual billing saves 10 percent.
Your invoices and payment history are available under Billing history, where you
can download a PDF of any past invoice.

If a payment fails, QuickBooks retries the charge after 3 days and sends an email
each time. After three failed attempts the subscription is paused until you
update the payment method. To reset your password, use Forgot password on the
sign-in screen; the reset link expires after 60 minutes.
