# QuickBooks: Creating and Sending Invoices

Source: QuickBooks Help (mock data for training)

To create an invoice, go to Sales, then Invoices, then New invoice. Choose a
customer, add products or services as line items, and set a due date. The
default payment term is Net 30, which means payment is due 30 days after the
invoice date.

You can email an invoice directly from QuickBooks or share a payment link.
Online payment links accept credit card and ACH bank transfer. The card
processing fee is 2.9 percent plus 25 cents per transaction, and the ACH fee is
1 percent capped at 10 dollars per transaction.

To turn on automatic payment reminders, go to Settings, Account and settings,
Sales, and enable Reminders. QuickBooks can send a reminder 7 days before the
due date, on the due date, and 7 days after.
