# QuickBooks: Sales Tax

Source: QuickBooks Help (mock data for training)

QuickBooks calculates sales tax automatically based on the customer address and
the products you sell. Turn it on from the Taxes menu by selecting Set up sales
tax and entering your business address.

The sample tax rates used in this course are: California 7.25 percent, New York
4.0 percent, Texas 6.25 percent, Washington 6.5 percent, and Florida 6.0
percent. These are state base rates and do not include local district taxes.

To find how much tax you owe, open the Taxes menu and view the Sales tax
liability report. You can record a tax payment from the same screen. Filing
frequency (monthly, quarterly, or annual) is set by your state tax agency.
