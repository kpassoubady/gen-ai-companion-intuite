### The Story

The autumn wind swirled golden leaves across the Manhattan pavement as Elias handed Clara her usual oat milk latte. "Ready for our tourist day?" he smiled. Clara, adjusting her signature red scarf, nodded eagerly. They descended into the clattering depths of the subway, standing shoulder-to-shoulder in the crowded Q train, sharing wired earphones and secret smiles as the city rushed by in a blur of steel and graffiti. Emerging at Battery Park, the crisp, salty air hit them. They boarded the ferry, leaning together against the cold metal railing. As the monumental copper torch of the Statue of Liberty came into view, Elias wrapped his arm around her, the towering New York skyline standing as a silent witness to their quiet romance. Later, walking through the echoing, sunlit halls of Ellis Island, they traced the bronze plaques on the immigrant wall. Elias found his great-grandfather’s name, squeezing Clara’s hand tightly. In that historic space, surrounded by the echoes of past journeys, they realized their own future was just beginning.

### The Master Prompt Strategy

To keep characters consistent across AI image generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion), you need a **Master Subject Block**—a rigid, highly specific description of the characters' physical features and clothing that you copy and paste into *every* prompt.

**Master Subject Block:**

> **Elias:** 30-year-old man, messy dark brown hair, gentle hazel eyes, light stubble, wearing a navy blue wool peacoat over a grey sweater.
> **Clara:** 28-year-old woman, shoulder-length wavy auburn hair, freckles, wearing a bright red knit scarf and a camel-colored trench coat.
> **Style:** Cinematic photography, 35mm lens, soft romantic autumn lighting, hyper-realistic, photorealistic.

---
